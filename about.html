About the mall

Opened in 1998, Pine City Mall has grown into the region's favourite spot to shop, eat and unwind. Under one skylit roof you'll find over 60 stores, a food hall, a six-screen cinema and a landscaped central atrium.

Our name comes from the stand of century-old pines preserved on the site — three of them still grow in the atrium today, right where the fountain stands.
